ScanFlow Documents

Privacy Policy · Effective October 13, 2026

Basic scanning and OCR are designed to run on your device. If cloud sync is enabled in the app release, documents are automatically synced to ScanFlow’s Supabase project. This policy explains local processing, online features, subscriptions, and analytics.

1. Who we are

ScanFlow Documents (“ScanFlow”, “we”, “us”) is a document scanning and organization app. This policy applies to the ScanFlow Documents iOS app, related services, and this website.

2. Information processed

Information processed on your device

  • Photos or camera captures that you select or scan.
  • OCR text, document titles, extracted dates, amounts, reference numbers, and reminders.
  • PDF, DOCX, PNG, and other files you open, create, edit, or export.

Basic scanning, Apple Vision OCR, document viewing, and basic PDF export are processed locally where supported. We do not receive the contents of files used only for these local features.

Information uploaded when you choose a cloud feature

  • When cloud sync is configured for the app release, the app automatically syncs a document image, title, OCR text, and document identifier to ScanFlow’s Supabase project.
  • AI summarize, rewrite, or translate sends the extracted text and requested language to our Supabase Edge Function and an AI provider so the requested result can be generated.
  • If high-fidelity cloud conversion is enabled in a release, the selected PDF may be sent to the conversion service. The app must disclose this before upload.

Do not use online AI or conversion features for information that you are not permitted to share.

Purchase and account information

Apple and RevenueCat process subscription transactions, purchase status, product identifiers, and subscription eligibility. We do not receive your full payment card number. Cloud sync creates an anonymous Supabase authentication session so documents can be associated with the same app installation. The current app does not provide a user account upgrade or cross-device identity recovery flow.

Optional analytics

If you enable “Share usage analytics”, ScanFlow may send an installation identifier and events such as scan completed, conversion paywall viewed, or purchase flow state. Analytics events do not include document images, OCR text, extracted field values, or file contents. You can disable this setting in the app.

3. How we use information

  • Provide scanning, OCR, file conversion, AI, reminders, sharing, and cloud features you request.
  • Process purchases, restore access, prevent duplicate entitlement errors, and provide support.
  • Diagnose reliability and improve the app when optional analytics are enabled.
  • Protect the service, detect abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell document contents or use your documents to build an advertising profile.

4. Sharing and service providers

We share information only with providers needed for the feature you use: Apple for App Store transactions, RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management, Supabase for optional authentication/storage/Edge Functions, the configured AI provider for user-requested AI text processing, and an optional conversion provider for user-requested cloud conversion. Providers process information under their own terms and applicable data-processing obligations.

5. Retention and deletion

Local documents remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Cloud-synced documents remain in ScanFlow’s Supabase project until you delete them in the app or the project administrator removes them. AI and conversion requests may be retained temporarily by the relevant provider for processing, security, or legal requirements under that provider’s policy. Delete synced documents in the app when you no longer want them stored online.

6. Security

We use platform permissions, authenticated requests, storage access policies, and encrypted HTTPS connections for supported online services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device and Apple account protected, and do not upload documents unless you have authorization to do so.

7. Your choices

  • Allow or deny camera, photo library, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Use local scanning and OCR without using AI or cloud conversion features. Cloud sync may be enabled automatically in a configured app release.
  • Disable analytics in ScanFlow Settings.
  • Delete documents in the app and manage subscriptions through Apple Account Settings.
  • Contact the support channel listed on the ScanFlow App Store page for privacy questions or requests.

8. Children

ScanFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact the support address listed on the App Store page.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ScanFlow’s features or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, use the support contact published on the ScanFlow Documents App Store listing and include “Privacy request” in the subject.
