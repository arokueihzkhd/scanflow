ScanFlow Documents

Marketing Policy · Effective October 13, 2026

This policy governs ScanFlow’s advertising, App Store messaging, promotional offers, lifecycle messages, and partner communications. We promote useful document workflows without hiding subscription terms or making unsupported accuracy claims.

1. Accurate product claims

Marketing must describe features that are available in the advertised version, region, device, and plan. Screenshots and demonstrations must represent the real ScanFlow experience. We do not claim that OCR, AI summaries, translations, or file conversion are perfect, legally binding, or suitable as a substitute for professional advice.

2. Subscription and trial disclosure

  • Promotions must clearly identify whether a feature requires ScanFlow Pro.
  • Prices and currencies must come from the applicable App Store listing or StoreKit product and may vary by region.
  • “7-day free trial” may be shown only when Apple reports that the selected user and product are eligible for that introductory offer.
  • Promotional copy must state the renewal price, renewal period, automatic renewal, and how to cancel before the charge.
  • We do not describe a paid subscription as free, lifetime, or one-time unless that is the actual product being sold.

3. Fair promotions

We may offer launch pricing, introductory trials, or limited promotional placements. A promotion must state its eligibility, start and end dates where applicable, and the normal renewal price. We do not use fake countdowns, fake scarcity, misleading “last chance” language, hidden buttons, or repeated interruptions to pressure a purchase.

4. User choice and cancellation

Users can continue to access free features without purchasing. Closing a paywall must not delete existing documents or reminders. Marketing messages must not obstruct basic scanning, OCR, document viewing, or basic PDF export. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Apple Account Settings.

5. Privacy in marketing

Marketing must not imply that documents are uploaded when a feature is local, or that all features are private when a user-selected AI, sync, or cloud conversion feature sends data online. We do not use document contents in advertising audiences or publish a user’s document, image, OCR text, or extracted field without permission.

6. Testimonials and comparisons

Testimonials must be genuine and not materially edited to change their meaning. Any comparison with another app must be current, supportable, and based on the same feature scope and price region. We do not buy fabricated reviews, impersonate users, or encourage reviews in exchange for payment or subscription access.

7. AI and document safety

Marketing may describe ScanFlow as helping users review dates, amounts, references, and next steps. It must also make clear that extracted fields and AI output should be checked against the original document. ScanFlow does not provide legal, medical, financial, immigration, or government advice.

8. Regional and accessibility considerations

Promotions must use the correct local language, currency, availability, and subscription disclosures. We aim to keep promotional text readable with assistive technologies and avoid images that contain the only important price or legal information.

9. Partners and affiliates

Partners must use approved product descriptions, current prices, and approved links. Paid or affiliate relationships must be disclosed where required. Partners may not promise guaranteed OCR accuracy, guaranteed savings, or a trial that is not offered by Apple for the user’s account.

10. Reporting concerns

To report an inaccurate promotion, misleading subscription message, or privacy concern, use the support contact published on the ScanFlow Documents App Store listing and include “Marketing policy” in the subject. We may correct or remove materials that do not follow this policy.
